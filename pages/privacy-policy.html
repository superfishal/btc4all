Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Bitcoin Lightning Network

Our services utilize Bitcoin's Lightning Network, which provides enhanced privacy through:

  • Decentralized payment routing
  • No central authority controlling transactions
  • Reduced transaction traceability compared to traditional payment systems
